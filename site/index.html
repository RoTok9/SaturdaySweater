The program

Everything but the playbook

A 136-school landscape with ten conferences, conference title games, a 12-team playoff with byes and home first-round games, rivalries you have to earn, and a Heisman race driven by real production.

RECRUITING

Win the living room

Text recruits, read their personality, pitch what they care about, and answer the family at dinner on the visit. Scholarships, NIL and a fund you have to raise yourself.

GAME DAY

Watch it play out, then make the call

Games play live with a drive-by-drive broadcast, a field that tracks the ball, weather, and a swinging win-probability meter. At halftime and again in a tight fourth quarter the broadcast stops and you make the call: open it up, bleed the clock, send the house, light into them. The rest of the game is simulated from your choice.

RIVALRIES

Earned, never given

Ranked matchups, close finishes, upsets and revenge games build heat between two programs. Enough heat and the series gets a trophy name and a spot in Rivalry Week. Lose three straight and the boosters get loud.

THE PODIUM

Face the press

After every game you pick an answer. Fans, the locker room, the athletic department and the media keep score, and it decides your hot seat and which jobs call.

THE ROSTER

Players are people

Traits, injuries, breakouts and busts. Leaders hold a locker room together, flight risks hit the portal, late bloomers pop in camp. Pin your own depth chart.

THE LEAGUE

It lives without you

Named coaches with their own seats, a coaching carousel every offseason, a hot seat index, a league history book, and rankings that weigh who you actually beat.

DECISIONS

Every move matters

Your agent calls about a bigger job midseason. TV wants your game on Friday night. A backup's dad threatens the portal. Academics flags a starter. Each answer ripples into next Saturday, your budget, the locker room and January.

THE OFFSEASON

December to camp

A portal window where your backups tell you they are leaving and you get one conversation to keep them. NIL retention asks from agents. A late signing period. Flip attempts on other programs' commits. A coaching tree that follows your assistants to their head jobs.

CO-OP

Play with friends

Pass-and-play on one device or an online league where the week does not advance until every coach has readied up. A league table tracks records, class ranks, hot seats and head-to-head.

Fourteen weeks

Each week: spend recruiting hours, lobby, then sim. Your game plays out live and ends at the podium.

The one that matters

Championship week

Conference title games. Your class signs. The Heisman is handed out. The 12-team field is set.

January
Playoff and offseason

Four rounds, one champion. Then departures, the portal, the coaching carousel, and the phone: job offers if you overachieved, a pink slip if you didn't.

Questions

From the mailbag

Are these real schools?

No. The league ships with 136 fictional programs that mirror a modern conference landscape. The League Editor lets you rename anything, and a league file can be shared with friends and imported in one paste.

Where is my save?

In the browser version, in that browser's local storage. In the Windows app, in your Windows user profile; File > Export save writes a backup file you can move to another PC. You can keep several dynasties and switch between them.

How do updates work?

The browser version is always current. The Windows app has Check for updates under Program > Save & settings; it downloads the new game file and reloads without touching your saves.

Is it hard?

Taking over a 10-prestige program is a title-or-bust job, and ranked teams get everyone's best shot. A 2-star program is a five-year project with a lot of losing in year one. Three losing seasons in a row and you're fired. Both are the point.

Why "Sweater"?

Sweating the games. If you've ever watched a fourth quarter through your fingers, you already know.
