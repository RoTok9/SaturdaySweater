Everything but the playbook

A full 136-school landscape with ten conferences, conference title games, a 12-team playoff with byes and home first-round games, and a Heisman race driven by real production.

RECRUITING

Win the living room

Every recruit wants three things: playing time, NIL, a contender, proximity, academics. Scout, call, pitch, host visits and write checks from a fund you have to raise yourself.

GAME DAY

Watch it play out

Games resolve live on screen: field goals, long touchdowns, fumble returns, a swinging win-probability meter and a full box score with player stats at the final.

THE PODIUM

Face the press

After every game you pick an answer. Fans, the locker room, the athletic department and the media all keep score, and it decides your hot seat and which jobs call.

THE POLLS

Resume matters

Rankings weigh who you beat. Ranked wins carry extra credit, losses to good teams cost less. Watch the projected bracket shift every week from October on.

THE ROSTER

Breakouts and busts

Players get hot, go cold, pop in camp, declare early, or hit the transfer portal when the locker room sours. Seniors leave, your class arrives, the cycle turns.

YOUR LEAGUE

Name your schools

A built-in editor renames any program, nickname or color, moves schools between conferences, and imports a whole league from a friend in one paste.

How a season goes

Preseason

Pick your program and your coach name. Breakouts emerge in camp. The AD tells you how many wins keep you employed.

Fourteen weeks

Each week: spend recruiting hours, lobby, then sim. Your game plays out live and ends at the podium.

Championship week

Conference title games. Your class signs. The Heisman is handed out. The 12-team field is set.

Playoff

Four rounds. Top seeds rest, everyone else hosts or travels. One team finishes No. 1.

Offseason

Departures, development, the portal, budget upgrades, and the phone: job offers if you overachieved, a pink slip if you didn't.

Questions

Are these real schools?

No. The league ships with 136 fictional programs that mirror a modern conference landscape. The League Editor lets you rename anything, and a league file can be shared with friends and imported in one paste.

Where is my save?

In the browser version, in that browser's local storage. In the Windows app, in your Windows user profile; File > Export save writes a backup file you can move to another PC.

How do updates work?

The browser version is always current. The Windows app has Check for updates under Program > Save & settings; it downloads the new game file and reloads without touching your save.

Is it hard?

Taking over a 10-prestige program is a title-or-bust job. A 2-star program is a five-year project with a lot of losing in year one. Both are the point.
