Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

The short version
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics. Your dynasty is saved on your own device.
  • If you join an online league, the league server stores what the league needs to run: the shared league save, the coach names you type and whose turn it is. That data is deleted when the league ends or goes quiet for 180 days.
  • The game does not show ads today. When optional rewarded ads arrive, Google will serve them, and personalized ads will only be shown if you allow them.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Who we are

Saturday Sweater: CFB Dynasty ("Saturday Sweater", "we", "us") is an independent game made by the developer of Saturday Sweater. This policy covers the website at saturdaysweater.com, the browser version of the game, the Windows app, the iPhone and Android apps, and the online league service (together, the "Services").

Questions or requests about your information: privacy@saturdaysweater.com.

2. Information that stays on your device

Everything you do in a dynasty (your saves, settings, coach name, notes, and any names you enter in the league editor) is stored on your device: in your browser's local storage on the web, in the app's data folder on Windows, and in the app's private storage on phones. The phone apps keep a second copy in the app's private files so a save survives if the phone clears web storage. None of this is sent to us.

You control it: delete a dynasty from the game's menu, clear the site's data in your browser, or uninstall the app. If you export a dynasty file, it goes wherever you save it.

3. Information we and our providers process

3.1 Visiting the website and downloading updates

The website and the Windows app's update files are hosted on GitHub Pages and GitHub. When you visit the site or the Windows app checks for an update, GitHub receives standard request information such as your IP address, browser or app type, and the time of the request. GitHub states that on GitHub Pages "the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes." The update check sends no game data. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Fonts are hosted on our own site, so no font requests go to third parties.

3.2 Online leagues (optional)

If you create or join an online league, the league server stores:

  • the league name and six-character league code;
  • the coach name you enter and which school you coach;
  • the shared league save, which includes every coach's program, results, recruiting and any notes coaches leave for each other;
  • each coach's pending week, ready status, and the times coaches joined and were last active;
  • a random access token, also saved on your device, that proves a request comes from your coach.

We use this only to run the league. Anyone who has the league code can view the league, so please do not put personal information in coach names, league names or notes.

The league server runs on Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare processes each request for us, including your IP address, to deliver it and protect the service. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

How long it is kept: a coach's data is removed when they leave the league or the commissioner removes them, and the whole league is deleted when the last coach leaves or after 180 days with no activity.

3.3 Rewarded ads (coming soon, optional)

Saturday Sweater does not show ads at the moment. We plan to offer an optional rewarded ad: you can choose to watch one ad a week to earn extra recruiting hours. Watching is always your choice, and the game is fully playable without it. When ads are turned on, this section applies:

  • Who serves them: on iPhone and Android, Google AdMob; on the web, Google's H5 Games Ads program through Google AdSense.
  • What they collect: Google and its partners may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on Android), IP address, device and browser information, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with the ad. On the web they use cookies and similar technologies.
  • Why: to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, cap how often you see an ad, and, only if you allow it, to personalize ads.
  • Cookies on the web: third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • Your choices: on iPhone, the app asks for permission before any tracking (App Tracking Transparency), and you can change it any time in Settings. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you will be asked for consent first. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings, opt out of other vendors' personalized ads at www.aboutads.info/choices, and reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device's settings. Non-personalized ads may still use cookies or identifiers for frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement.
  • More detail: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If we add any other ad network, we will name it here, with a link to its opt-out, before it is used.

3.4 When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and keep it only as long as needed to handle your message.

3.5 Things you choose to share

Buttons like "Copy result" and the season share card put text or an image on your clipboard or in your device's share sheet. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you send it yourself.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts or precise location to play.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools in the game or on the website.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising except through the optional ads described in section 3.3, with your choices respected.

5. Who we share information with

Only the service providers named above: GitHub (website and update hosting), Cloudflare (online leagues), and, once ads are live, Google (advertising). Apple and Google also deliver the phone apps through their stores under their own privacy policies. We only use providers whose terms require them to protect personal information at least as well as this policy describes. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of players or the Services.

6. International transfers

Our providers operate in the United States and other countries. When information about players in the EEA, UK or Switzerland is transferred, it is protected by the safeguards those providers offer, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

We process league data because it is necessary to provide the online league you asked to join (performance of a contract), and website and server logs for security and to keep the Services running (legitimate interests). Personalized ads rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

9. Your rights and how to use them

Depending on where you live (for example, the EEA, UK, Switzerland, California and other US states with privacy laws), you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct it or delete it;
  • object to or restrict how we use it;
  • withdraw consent;
  • opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising (we do not sell it, and the ad choices in section 3.3 cover targeted ads).

You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. We will not treat you differently for using any of these rights.

Most of your data is already in your hands: delete a dynasty in the game, or leave an online league from the league bar. For anything else, email privacy@saturdaysweater.com. Since there are no accounts, we may ask for the league code and coach name so we can find the right data. We will respond within 30 days, or sooner if the law requires it.

10. Children

Saturday Sweater is a general-audience sports management game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Before ads are turned on, the game will ask players their age in a neutral way. Players under 13, and players under 16 in the EEA and UK, will not be shown personalized ads, and their advertising identifier will not be used for advertising. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, for example in an online league, email us and we will delete it.

11. Security

All connections to the website and the league server use HTTPS. League access tokens are random and are checked on every request. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we keep what we hold to the minimum the game needs.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For significant changes, such as turning on ads, we will also tell you in the game's What's new notes before the change takes effect.
